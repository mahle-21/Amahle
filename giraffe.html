GIRAFFE

Giraffes are the tallest land animals, known for their long necks, legs, and unique spotted coats. They use their height to browse on treetops, especially acacia leaves.

They are social animals often seen in groups called towers and communicate using body language and low-frequency sounds.

WHERE WILL YOU FIND THEM

You will find the giraffes at pen G10, near the savanna viewing deck.
